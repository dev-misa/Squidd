Everything is now Squidd
------------------------
- Repo folder:          ~/Squidd

- Target and scheme:    Squidd
- Source folder:        Squidd/
- App entry file:       Squidd/SquiddApp.swift (struct SquiddApp)

Side effects of changing the bundle identifier
----------------------------------------------
macOS treats com.squidd as a brand-new app, so:
- Settings start fresh (defaults are stored per bundle identifier).
- Spotify must be reconnected once (tokens are in a new keychain service).
- The app gets a new sandbox container.

Leftovers already deleted (2026-09-11):
- ~/Library/Preferences/com.misaspace-macos.plist (unused; sandboxed app reads its container)
- ~/Library/Preferences/MisaSpace.AuthChecks.*.plist (empty, from old check runs)
- /tmp/misaspace-* build output, check binaries, Swift cache, logs, phase1-4 staging copies
- ~/Library/Developer/Xcode/DerivedData/misaspace-macos-*
- Application Support/misaspace/misaspace-data.json.corrupt-* (stale broken backup)

/Applications/Squidd.app replaced with the com.squidd build (2026-09-11).
Spotify client ID, window position, outline setting and custom mascot were
copied into ~/Library/Containers/com.squidd. Spotify needs one reconnect, and
Launch at Login must be turned back on from the new app's menu.
Deleted (no longer used by anything):
- Keychain item com.misaspace-macos.spotify
- Everything inside ~/Library/Containers/com.misaspace-macos
- ~/Library/Application Scripts/com.misaspace-macos
- ~/Library/Caches/com.misaspace-macos
- ~/Library/HTTPStorages/com.misaspace-macos
- Xcode archive "misaspace-macos 9-11-26, 10.40 AM" (build of the old app)

macOS protects the container's metadata file, so an empty
~/Library/Containers/com.misaspace-macos folder may remain. If it's still
there, drag it to the Trash in Finder.

Belongs to the old Electron app (~/misaspace), kept while that app exists:
- ~/Library/Application Support/misaspace (ILLIT group/bias setup, ink log, tokens)
- Keychain item "misaspace Safe Storage" (decrypts the Electron Spotify tokens)
