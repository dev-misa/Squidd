HOW TO SHARE SQUIDD (macOS app) WITH A FRIEND

Context:
- App name: Squidd (Xcode project: Squidd.xcodeproj)
- Native macOS SwiftUI app, App Sandbox enabled, automatic code signing
- Minimum macOS version: macOS 26 (Tahoe). The friend's Mac must run macOS 26 or newer.
- The app is NOT notarized, so macOS will show a security warning the first time it opens.
- The app uses Spotify. The friend needs access to a Spotify developer app (mine or their own).
- Spotify redirect URI: http://127.0.0.1:8888/callback
- Spotify login uses a Client ID only (PKCE, no client secret).


PART 1 - BUILD A COPY OF THE APP (on my Mac)
1. Open Squidd.xcodeproj in Xcode.
2. At the top of the window, set the run destination to "My Mac".
3. In the menu bar, click Product > Archive and wait for the build to finish. The Organizer window opens on its own.
4. Select the new archive and click "Distribute App".
5. Choose "Custom", then "Copy App", then "Next", and save it somewhere easy like the Desktop.
6. Open the saved folder. Inside is Squidd.app. Right-click it and choose "Compress Squidd" to get Squidd.zip.


PART 2 - SEND IT
7. Send Squidd.zip through AirDrop, Google Drive, iCloud Drive, Discord or similar.


PART 3 - FRIEND OPENS IT
8. Double-click the zip to unzip it, then drag Squidd.app into the Applications folder.
9. Double-click Squidd. macOS says it "can't be opened" or "can't verify the developer." Click "Done" (NOT "Move to Trash").
10. Go to System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, click "Open Anyway" next to Squidd, and confirm with the Mac password.
11. Squidd now opens normally every time.


PART 4 - SPOTIFY ACCESS (pick one)

Option A - Use my Spotify app (easier for the friend):
1. Go to https://developer.spotify.com/dashboard and open my app.
2. Open Settings > User Management. Add my friend's name and the email they use for Spotify.
3. Send them my Client ID. It's safe to share, since the app doesn't use a secret.
4. They open Squidd Settings, paste the Client ID, click "Connect Spotify", and log in.

Option B - Friend makes their own Spotify app:
1. They create an app at https://developer.spotify.com/dashboard
2. They add this redirect URI exactly: http://127.0.0.1:8888/callback (must be 127.0.0.1, not localhost)
3. They paste their own Client ID into Squidd Settings and click "Connect Spotify".
4. The Spotify account that owns the developer app needs Premium.


GOOD TO KNOW
- Updates: every new version means repeating Parts 1-3.
- Skipping the security warning: with a paid Apple Developer account ($99/year), choose "Direct Distribution" in step 5 instead of "Copy App". Xcode then notarizes the app with Apple, and the friend can open it without steps 9-10.
- Spotify user limit: while my Spotify app is in development mode, only the few people I add in User Management can connect.
- If Spotify login fails because port 8888 is busy, quit whatever app is using that port (like the old Electron app) and try again.
